Why an AI cofounder

A cofounder does more than answer. Minaco keeps the decision moving.

Search gives you information. A generic assistant gives you an answer. Minaco works from your context, challenges the decision, and carries the next step forward.

01

Shared context, not repeated prompts.

Minaco remembers what you are building, your stage, constraints, preferences, and prior decisions so each discussion starts further ahead.

02

Judgment before execution.

It surfaces assumptions, tradeoffs, and missing evidence before recommending a move. You keep the final call; Minaco makes the call better informed.

03

From decision to action.

When the work needs research, connected data, outreach, or a concrete deliverable, Minaco can carry it forward inside the same discussion.

04

Continuity across the company-building loop.

Competitive insight can shape positioning, positioning can shape first-user outreach, and the results can improve the next decision without losing the thread.
